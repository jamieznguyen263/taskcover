# Conversion Path Review

Task 13B review of public CTAs and buyer paths.

## Primary Conversion Paths

- Diagnostic intent: localized `/free-seo-audit`.
- Strategy/fit intent: localized `/book-a-call`.
- General or partner/media intent: localized `/contact`.
- Private reference intent: localized `/contact?intent=private-reference`.
- Privacy/data intent: localized `/data-request`.

## Current Strengths

- Services and industry pages generally use Free SEO Audit as primary and
  Book Strategy Call as secondary.
- Market, PPC, mentor, Work, and Proof paths include strategy or reference
  CTAs where appropriate.
- Contact exposes Data Request as privacy context without turning it into a
  sales path.
- Thank-you remains noindex and excluded from sitemap.
- Lead forms do not claim success unless an adapter accepts delivery.

## Current Weaknesses

- Header navigation does not guide visitors by decision path. It exposes page
  families but not buyer intent.
- Proof is a top-level nav item, which makes Work vs Proof feel separate even
  though Proof supports Work and trust validation.
- Footer has many useful links but old grouping increases scanning effort.
- Homepage dashboard supports the value proposition visually, but it does not
  yet show the action queue and revenue path that make conversion logic clear.
- Homepage hero proof line points to selected experience rather than the new
  verified case-study system.

## CTA Principles For This Task

- Header CTA: Free SEO Audit.
- Services: Free SEO Audit first; Book Strategy Call second.
- Industries: Free SEO Audit first; Book Strategy Call second.
- Markets: Book Strategy Call may be emphasized where market-expansion fit is
  the buyer question, with Free SEO Audit still available.
- Work and case studies: Free SEO Audit, Book Strategy Call, or Request Private
  Reference depending on context.
- Proof: Book Strategy Call and Request Private Reference.
- Insights: Related service and Free SEO Audit.
- Company/methodology/how-we-work: Book Strategy Call and Free SEO Audit.
- Legal/privacy/data pages: Contact and Data Request only where appropriate.

## Localization

All CTA labels, contextual menu CTA copy, dashboard labels, and mobile menu
labels must be localized in EN/FR/ES. Public FR/ES pages must not receive new
hardcoded English strings.

## Future Items Not Built Now

- Analytics events, GA4, GTM, Google Ads, remarketing, or consent banner.
- New SEM-specific landing pages.
- New commercial route variants.
- New testimonial or proof capture flows.

## Task 13B Outcome

- Header now keeps Free SEO Audit as the single global primary CTA.
- Services mega-menu includes a contextual Free SEO Audit CTA for diagnostic
  intent.
- Work mega-menu includes the private-reference intent path:
  `/contact?intent=private-reference`.
- Footer exposes Free SEO Audit, Book a Call, Contact, Data Request, and Legal
  links in clearer groups.
- Homepage cockpit now includes a conversion path rail and sprint queue, so
  the hero visual supports action prioritization rather than passive reporting.
- Homepage hero proof line now points visitors toward verified case-study
  coverage without implying endorsement from brand names.
