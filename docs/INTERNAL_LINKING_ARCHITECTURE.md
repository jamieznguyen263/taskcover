# Internal Linking Architecture

The internal linking system should reinforce the primary URL for each keyword family without adding repetitive link blocks everywhere.

## Core Linking Rules

- Link from informational pages to the relevant commercial service page when the article explains a problem the service solves.
- Link from service pages to related proof, sample audits, and case studies where the relationship is real.
- Link from industry pages to recommended service modules and relevant case studies.
- Link from market pages to international SEO, local SEO, PPC, and vertical pages only where the market content supports it.
- Do not add links solely to repeat keywords.
- Do not make proof pages look like commercial landing pages.
