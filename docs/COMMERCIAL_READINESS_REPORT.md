# Commercial Readiness Report

## 1. Summary

Taskcover has enough commercial SEO architecture for launch without creating new pages. The correct action is consolidation: one keyword family, one primary URL, supported by services, industries, markets, proof, sample audits, Insights, and lead funnels.

## 2. Existing URL Count

The latest known build generated 260 static pages. Task 13 did not add new public routes, so the expected static page count remains 260 pending final build output.

## 3. Keyword Family Coverage

Covered families include core agency terms, SEO audit, technical SEO, AI Search/GEO, content marketing, digital PR, local SEO, international SEO, PPC, SEO mentor/advisory, USA/Canada/Australia market SEO, priority industries, proof, case studies, and sample audits.

## 7. Cannibalization Risks

Main risks are homepage vs SEO agency, service hub vs service detail pages, audit service vs free audit funnel, service pages vs Insights articles, and international SEO vs market pages. These are documented and manageable with canonical intent ownership.

## 8. Missing Proof Risks

Some proof paths are strong, especially case studies and sample audits. Digital PR, AI Search/GEO, SEO Mentor, and some verticals would benefit from more specific proof over time.

## 9. SEM Readiness

No ads are active. No tracking scripts were added. SEO Agency and SEO Audit pages are closest to future SEM-ready, but all SEM activation should wait for consent, tracking events, and conversion reporting.

## 10. Internal Linking Improvements

The internal linking plan maps each commercial page to supporting services, industries, markets, proof, sample audits, Insights, and lead CTAs. The Spanish footer cleanup improved navigation readability.
