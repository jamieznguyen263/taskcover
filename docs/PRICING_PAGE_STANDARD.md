# Pricing Page Standard

Taskcover publishes one localized pricing system at `/pricing`, `/fr/pricing`,
and `/es/pricing`. English slugs remain canonical for every locale.

## Pricing Model

All pricing is shown in USD and must be described as a starting point. Final
pricing depends on market, site complexity, competition, content needs,
execution speed, internal support, and whether the work is advisory or
implementation-led.

Core starting points:

- Free SEO Audit: $0.
- Local SEO Starter: from $1,000/month.
- Additional locations: from $300-$500/location/month.
- National SEO Foundation: from $2,500/month.
- National SEO Growth System: from $4,500/month and recommended for execution.
- Global SEO Expansion: from $7,500/month.
- Enterprise Authority System: custom from $12,000/month.

SEO Mentor starting points:

- SEO Mentor Office Hours: from $1,000/month.
- SEO Mentor Growth Advisory: from $1,500/month and recommended for advisory.
- SEO Mentor Team Enablement: from $2,500/month.
- Executive Search Advisory: from $4,500/month.
- Custom SEO Mentorship: custom.

Audits and add-ons:

- Search Growth Audit: from $1,500 one-time.
- Technical SEO Audit: from $2,500 one-time.
- AI Search Visibility Review: from $1,500 one-time.
- PPC + Organic Intelligence Review: from $2,500 one-time.
- International SEO Market Map: from $3,500 one-time.
- PPC Management: from $1,500/month or 10-12% of ad spend, whichever is higher.
- Ad spend is not included.

## Scope Rules

Lower entry plans are intentionally focused. They must not be presented as
full-service SEO campaigns. Content writing, development implementation,
translation production, CRM implementation, ad spend, PR placement fees,
guaranteed backlinks, and guaranteed rankings are excluded unless explicitly
scoped.

Enterprise, migration, regulated, multi-location, multilingual, and complex
multi-market work requires custom pricing after diagnostic review.

## Recommendation Rules

Recommend Free SEO Audit when the visitor is unsure, needs a starting diagnosis,
or needs the smallest realistic scope identified before a quote.

Recommend Strategy Call when the visitor already has a market, budget, team, or
timeline question that needs senior judgment.

## SEO And Schema

Pricing pages emit localized metadata, canonical URLs, hreflang alternates,
BreadcrumbList schema, and FAQPage schema only because the FAQ is visible on the
page. Do not add Review, AggregateRating, LocalBusiness, or unsafe pricing
schema.

## Future SEM Notes

The pricing page can support SEM traffic, but future dedicated SEM landing
pages must remain distinct from organic pricing. They need localized copy,
consent/tracking readiness, proof support, and no fake scarcity, discounts, or
guaranteed outcomes.

## Task 15 QA Notes

  canonical.
- Language switching preserves safe `tab` values only.
- Full comparison is collapsed by default with native `details`.
- Tab rail uses horizontal overflow on mobile and fixed-width tab buttons.
- Context comparison changes by active tab.
- SEO Mentor and Audits tabs remain first-class service tabs.
- Custom Pricing remains outside the tab panel and always visible.
