# Insights Article Conversion Rail

Task 13C upgrades article pages from a left-TOC / center-content / passive-right-rail layout to a three-column reading experience.

## Desktop

- Left rail: sticky table of contents.
- Center: article body with a readable max width.
- Right rail: sticky conversion modules.

Right rail modules:

- Free audit CTA.
- Related service from article metadata.
- Related sample audit from article metadata.

## Mobile

The right rail is hidden on mobile. The same CTA/service/sample modules stack after the mobile table of contents, before the main article body.

## Table Overflow

Article comparison tables must use contained horizontal scroll inside the article body. They must not create body-level horizontal overflow.

## Not Changed

No analytics, tracking scripts, cookie banner, route changes, canonical changes, or sitemap changes were added.
