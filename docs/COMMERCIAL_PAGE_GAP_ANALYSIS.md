## Gap Conclusion

No new pages are required before launch. The site already has canonical commercial pages for all major proposed page types. Creating root-level duplicates now would create cannibalization and thin-page risk.

## Future Considerations

- A distinct SEO consulting page may be worth external keyword research after launch.
- A distinct GEO page may be worth considering only if search demand and buyer language separate from AI Search.
- SEM-specific landing pages should wait until tracking, consent, campaign architecture, and conversion event definitions are ready.
