# About Story Standard

This document governs the public About page at `/about`, `/fr/about`, and
`/es/about`.

## Confirmed Story

- Taskcover began in 2017 in Southeast Asia as a white-label search execution
  team.
- In 2018, the team built repeatable SEO delivery systems.
- In 2019, founder Jamiez Nguyen expanded the agency's commercial direction
  toward Canada and the United States.
- Australia is part of today's public market positioning, not a confirmed 2019
  milestone.
- Taskcover evolved from behind-the-scenes white-label execution into a public
  specialist SEO agency.
- Public brand: Taskcover Agency.
- Formal presentation: Taskcover Agency by Stoa Global Corporation.
- Legal/operator: Stoa Global Corporation.
- Public markets today: USA, Canada, and Australia.

## Leadership

Only these public leadership entries are confirmed:

- Jamiez Nguyen — Founder & CEO.
- John Edward — CTO.

Do not add other founders, staff, headcount, locations, awards, credentials,
education, social profiles, or links unless verified and approved.

## Portrait Rules

The About page reserves image-ready portrait slots for:

- `/team/jamiez-nguyen.webp`
- `/team/john-edward.webp`

Until real approved photos are provided, the page must render intentional
placeholders. Do not use stock headshots, fake portraits, AI-generated
portraits, or broken image tags.

## Methodology Story

The methodology section may explain how verified case-study work shaped
Taskcover's operating model. It may reference existing case-study slugs only.

Do not assign case studies to exact company history years unless the date is
already verified in the case-study record and needed in that specific context.

## Public Claim Limits

The About page must not claim:

- Guaranteed SEO rankings, traffic, leads, or revenue.
- Best agency, top agency, awards, ratings, reviews, or certifications.
- Physical offices or branches beyond the confirmed company address.
- Unverified founders, staff, team size, locations, client years, or social
  profiles.
- Fake quotes, fake logos, fake portraits, or invented client proof.

## SEO and Localization

The page uses English slugs across locales:

- `/about`
- `/fr/about`
- `/es/about`

Content, metadata, CTA labels, breadcrumb labels, timeline text, leadership
descriptions, and accessibility labels must remain localized in EN/FR/ES.
