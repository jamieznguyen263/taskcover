# Taskcover Agency — Project Brief

## 1. Project name

**Taskcover Agency**

Brand tagline: _Search Growth Agency for Google, AI Search, and Revenue._

## 2. Brand positioning

Taskcover Agency helps ambitious brands grow organic visibility, build authority,
improve AI search readiness, and convert high-intent search demand into
measurable business outcomes.

We position SEO, GEO (generative engine optimization), AEO (answer engine
optimization), content, and authority as **one connected search growth system**,
not isolated deliverables. Success is measured against visibility, trust,
leads, and revenue — not rankings alone.

### Main headline direction

> _SEO Built for Google, AI Search, and Revenue Growth._

## 6. Proof assets (planned)

The following proof surfaces are **intended** for the site but must only be
populated with **real, permissioned** assets:

- Selected team and partner brand experience (Agoda, Skyscanner, British
  Council, Avis, and others)
- Press / media features
- Client video reviews
- Spokesperson introduction videos
- Written client quotes
- Case studies with verified, attributable metrics

## 7. Brand credibility rules (very important)

- **No fake claims.** Do not invent awards, rankings guarantees, or outcomes.
- **No fake metrics.** Case study numbers must be attributable and verified.
- **No fake testimonials.** Only use real, permissioned quotes with verifiable
  attribution.
- **No fake press.** Only link to real, attributable articles.
- **Careful wording for brand experience.** Use phrasing such as:
  - _“Selected team and partner experience includes...”_
  - _“Experience across global brands and partners including...”_
- **Never use “trusted by”** unless real permission and direct proof exist.
- **No fake review schema and no spammy aggregate ratings.**

## 8. No fake claims rule (operational)

Until verified data is provided, the codebase uses clearly-labeled placeholders
(e.g. `—`, `Coming soon`, `Placeholder`, `Contact for details`). See
[`SEO_STANDARDS.md`](./SEO_STANDARDS.md) for the full schema policy.
