## Metrics Handling

All public metrics were carried as verified published results. Baselines were
recorded only where the source provided a baseline. Measurement platforms were
not invented. Timeframes were recorded only where the source included them.

## Asset Handling

Approved Webflow source images were downloaded locally, converted to WebP, and
stored under `public/case-studies/{slug}/`. Each case uses exactly two images.

## Prohibited Public Wording

Public case-study content must not expose or imply:

- Innovative Brain Agency
- iBrain
- partner agency work
- historical team experience
- unsupported endorsements or ratings schema
